Services

I test your network or web application the same way an attacker would — finding weaknesses before someone else does. You get a full report of what I found, how serious each issue is, and what to fix first.
  • Network and web application testing
  • Full written report with severity ratings
  • Plain-language executive summary
  • Fix recommendations included

Phishing Simulation and Staff Training
I send realistic phishing emails to your team to see who clicks, then run a training session explaining what to look for and how to respond. Available in Arabic or English.

  • Staff take-home awareness guide
JOD 80 to 200 per session
Security Audit
A review of your business's current security setup — passwords, access controls, software patching, backups, and how your team handles sensitive information. Good for businesses that want to know where they stand before investing further.

  • Recommended action plan
  • Follow-up consultation included
JOD 100 to 300 per audit
